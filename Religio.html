Religio

B 1 Graphic Identity

A conceptual book and folder system that treats the word Religio as an object, using red, black, and white to stage meaning like an exhibit.

01

Object

The book form becomes the main carrier, turning definition text and packaging structure into the visual identity.

02

Contrast

Red surfaces, black voids, and white plinths give the project a museum-like tension between belief and archive.

03

Edition

Repeated covers and folder variations make the language feel collectible, systematic, and slightly ritual.

Religio turns definition, material, and repetition into a quiet editorial artifact.
